Basement math

The basement smells like a cave, the dehumidifier tank is always full, and the thing hums all night on the power bill. Basement math turns floor area and ceiling into the liters of moisture to pull each day, tank size into hours between empties, and watts and hours into the kWh and cost of a dry year. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Damp plan - area and ceiling become cubic meters and liters a day.
  • Tank plan - tank size and daily liters become hours between empties.
  • Cost plan - watts and hours become kWh and cost per year.

Labeled norms

Labeled moisture load: 0.05 liters per cubic meter a day for a damp basement. Labeled ceiling: 2.2 m. Labeled tank: 4 L. Labeled unit: 300 W, 12 hours a day. Labeled tariff: 0.15 per kWh. These are reference values, not rules - your basement, your unit and your weather decide the real numbers.
